App Store Radar

What's actually growing on the Shopify App Store

Shopify shows you an app's rating and review count today. It keeps no history of either. So nobody can tell you which apps are accelerating, which are dying, or how fast a category is filling up — because that data has never existed anywhere. We started recording it.

The catalogue is a graveyard. The visible market isn't.

These two numbers describe the same store and look nothing alike — which is the whole point.

Why this can't be copied

  • The input is elapsed time. A competitor starting six months from now starts six months behind and never catches up. No amount of funding or AI compresses that.
  • Shopify publishes no history. Rank, rating and review count are available only as of right now. Yesterday is gone the moment it passes.
  • Every day widens the gap. The archive appends automatically and costs nothing to hold.
